About A.R.S Infratech

A house of discerning taste, built on a foundation of trust.

A.R.S Infratech is a Bengaluru-based real estate and construction consultancy serving aspirational investors, end-buyers and business leaders. We curate India’s finest residential and commercial opportunities — partnering with the country’s most respected developers — and deliver bespoke construction services with uncompromising craftsmanship.

Whether you are acquiring a home in a marquee gated community or commissioning a build-to-suit villa, our advisors, architects and engineers steward your journey with quiet rigour, transparent pricing and absolute discretion.
